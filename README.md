# 🎮 My Arcade Game Assignment

Welcome! Follow these steps to build your game, save your progress, and show off your work.

---

### 🚀 1. How to Start Coding
1.  **Copy the URL** of this GitHub page (from your browser's address bar).
2.  Go to [MakeCode Arcade](https://arcade.makecode.com/).
3.  Click the **Import** button (next to "New Project").
4.  Select **Import URL**.
5.  **Paste** your link and click **Import**.

### 💾 2. How to Save (Submit)
Make sure you save your work back to GitHub so your teacher can see it:
1.  In the MakeCode editor, click the **GitHub icon** (the cat logo) at the bottom.
2.  If it asks you to sign in, follow the prompts.
3.  Click **Commit & Push** (the up arrow icon) to save your changes.

### 🌐 3. How to Play Your Game Online
Since you have a school account, you can create a link to play your game in any browser:
1.  On **this** GitHub page, click the **Settings** tab at the top.
2.  On the left-hand menu, click **Pages**.
3.  Under **Build and deployment**, look for "Branch."
4.  Change the dropdown from *None* to **main** and click **Save**.
5.  Wait about a minute, refresh the page, and a link will appear at the top to play your game!
